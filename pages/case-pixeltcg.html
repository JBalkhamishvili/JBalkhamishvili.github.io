PixelTCG — WooCommerce Store

Lead WordPress developer (theme & plugin dev)

WordPressPHPJSWooCommerceACF

Overview

Pokémon TCG shop using a dark Astra child theme with custom category tiles, product blueprint pages, and automatic USD→GEL pricing via NBG API.

Problems

  • Inconsistent product titles/meta
  • Manual GEL price updates
  • Slow LCP on category/landing templates

Actions

  • Blueprint product template system for single-card pages
  • ACF USD field → GEL conversion hook (NBG API) feeding Woo price
  • Lazy-loaded sliders, trimmed plugins, improved image handling

Results

  • LCP: ↓ ~4.5s → ~2.3s on key templates
  • Admin time: ↓ manual price work ≈90%
  • SEO: cleaner slugs/titles across products
